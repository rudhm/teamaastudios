Is Team AA SFX an official Adobe product?

No. Team AA SFX is an independent plugin made by Team AA Studios for Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects. Adobe, Premiere Pro and After Effects are trademarks of Adobe.

What does the Mac installer do?

Extract the ZIP, close Adobe apps and run Install-Mac.command. It copies the panel to your per-user CEP extensions folder, backs up an existing version, and enables the required unsigned-panel preference for your account.

Where do my sound files go?

Nowhere. Your files stay on your Mac and are never uploaded. The panel stores only your folder, tag, favorite and preview preferences in your local Team AA SFX application support folder.
