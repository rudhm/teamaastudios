Is AA Flow an official Adobe product?

No. AA Flow is an independent plugin made by Team AA Studios for Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects. Adobe, Premiere Pro and After Effects are trademarks of Adobe.

What apps does it support?

The Studio toolkit is designed for Premiere Pro and After Effects 2024 or newer on macOS and Windows. Final compatibility details will ship with the first release.
